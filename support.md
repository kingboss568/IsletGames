## Purchase Help

Islet Games uses Apple StoreKit 2 for in-app purchases. If a purchase does not unlock immediately, open the unlock screen and tap "恢復購買". If the issue continues, include your device model, iOS version, and a short description when contacting support.

## Gameplay Help

The current release includes 10 casual games. Three games are free to play, and seven premium games are unlocked by the non-consumable "unlock all" purchase. Core gameplay and saved progress are designed to work offline.
