# Privacy Policy - Islet Games

Last updated: 2026-06-05

Islet Games is a local-first iPhone and iPad casual game bundle. The current release does not create accounts, does not run third-party advertising, does not use analytics SDKs, and does not collect personal data from players.

## Data Stored On Device

The app stores game progress, high scores, selected theme, onboarding completion, and collection-card unlocks locally on the device using SwiftData and UserDefaults. This information is used only to restore gameplay state inside the app.

## Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple StoreKit 2. The app reads verified StoreKit entitlements to unlock premium games and theme packs. Payment information is processed by Apple and is not collected or stored by Islet Games.

## Network And AI Features

The game can be played offline. Optional AI gameplay tips fall back to the message "AI 功能目前不可用" when the on-device Apple feature is unavailable. AI tips do not calculate scores, purchases, unlocks, or player rankings.

## Ads And Tracking

The current release does not include Google Mobile Ads, third-party advertising, cross-app tracking, or tracking domains. If advertising or tracking technology is added in a later version, this policy and the App Store privacy labels must be updated before release.

## Children

Islet Games is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
